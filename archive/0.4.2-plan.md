# 0.4.2 — deterministic rival pressure

Capture-and-hold tasks can run with two scripted rivals that continually press the center ring. The challenge is optional for normal scene browsing and is enabled by generated tasks. Rival pigment enters the existing field, so gas transport, pair permeability, reactions, caps, ring ownership and continuous share scoring decide what happens; no objective counters are written by the rivals.

## pressure sources

- `rivalPressure` is a 0/1 rule, default off in every scene profile.
- Each rival owns a fixed circular pressure site of radius 4 cells (49 cells), entirely inside the objective ring. For the 24-cell ring, the site center is 19 cells from the center.
- Sites lie midway between the active pigment's and each rival's deterministic color sector. Sector angles are -90°, 30° and 150° for R, G and B; each color therefore presses from the two neighboring fronts.
- Every simulation tick, each site adds 2 pigment units per cell per frame, bounded by the normal per-cell cap. The regular field analysis and cap pass run immediately after injection; transport, depletion, production and reactions then proceed in their usual order.
- The two sites are recalculated when the scene resets or the active pigment changes. Placement is formulaic and does not search the map or build a distance field.
- Pressure stops while paused, when the option is off, and after the objective is held. Disabling it stops only future injected mass; existing pigment and scene production remain until the map resets.

## task and sharing behavior

- The objective task row has a `rivals off/on` toggle. It restarts attempt statistics and the source placement, leaves the map intact, and saves the rule to the share URL.
- `new task` enables rival pressure after selecting its scene, pigment and hold target. `reset GUI` keeps the scene and restores its default, pressure-off rules.
- URL loading applies the scene profile before the validated pressure override. The default-off value is omitted from shared links.
- The existing ring score accumulates active-player share continuously as share-weighted seconds, including partial control. The measurement axis uses this alongside hold time, control time and peak share so pressure effects are visible before the 55% gate is crossed.

## measurement

`experiments/objective-bench.js` gives every run the same radius-8 opening claim, player R, six deterministic scenes, a 20-second target and a 600-tick (10-second) run. Rival-specific axes compare pressure off/on, pressure speed 20/90 with pressure on, and permeability closed/open with pressure on.

Observed spreads across the six scenes:

| axis | mean hold spread | mean control spread | mean peak-share spread | mean continuous-score spread | sensitive |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rival pressure off/on | 1.8 s | 0.0 s | 0.7 pp | 1.5 s | yes |
| pressure speed with rivals | 1.8 s | 0.6 s | 3.0 pp | 1.8 s | yes |
| permeability with rivals | 2.0 s | 0.4 s | 2.5 pp | 2.0 s | yes |

Sensitivity uses the existing 1-second control/score or 5-point peak-share threshold. Effects are scene-dependent: the largest off/on control-time change is 2.6 s; the largest pressure-speed score spread is 3.0 s; the largest permeability score spread is 5.4 s. The automatic suggestion already exposes pressure and permeability, so its rule set does not change.

## validation

- `experiments/rival-checks.js`: off mode, pressure footprint inside the ring, color-relative placement, removal of future supply when disabled, stop on win, and per-cell caps.
- `experiments/objective-bench.js 600 rival-pressure`, `600 pressure-with-rivals`, and `600 rival-mixing`: fixed-key scene measurements above.
- `experiments/dom-smoke.js`: default-off UI, generated-task auto-enable, reset-to-default, toggle and URL round trip.
- Manual pass: boot with pressure off; toggle on and watch the two colored fronts grow inside the ring; pause and resume; switch pigment and confirm the two rival colors change; disable pressure and verify existing pigment remains; generate a task, then reset GUI; reload a shared pressure-on URL.
