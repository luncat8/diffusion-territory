# 0.2.1 — mixable gas interdiffusion, reactive fronts, and total color budget

Refines staggered gas territory (`128×128`, players R, G, B on planar Q12.12 `Int32Array`) so mixing rules, reactive fronts, source placement in enemy territory, and optional total-color budgets behave smoothly and predictably.

## mixing and reaction (`gas.js`)

- **Unmixable rule (`!mixable || mixRate == 0`, default)**: Gases cannot enter cells occupied by other colors; they interact through shared total pressure `ptot = R + G + B` across a 1-cell VOF interface. Active source cells expel any displaced foreign species first (`pickDonorSpecies`), so painting a new source inside enemy territory purges foreign color cleanly to `0` while conserving 100% of foreign mass.
- **Mixable rule (`mixable && mixRate > 0`)**: Per-species face fluxes accelerate on `ch[c]` differences. Across faces carrying foreign gas `other = (ptot[a] - ra) + (ptot[b] - rb)`, smooth weight `f = min(1, other / (32 * FP_ONE))` interpolates `(damp, k)` toward Maxwell-Stefan drag `damp * (0.75 + 0.25 * perm)` and conductance `k * perm` (`perm = mixRate / 100`). Empty/own-color faces propagate at full wave speed; foreign faces scale monotonically with `mixRate` without tie discontinuities or `(pi, pi)` checkerboards.
- **Multi-species reaction (`Gas.react`)**: Sorts cell channels `v0 <= v1 <= v2`, removes `max(1, trunc(v0 * rate))` from all three channels when `v0 > 0`, and removes `max(1, trunc((v1 - v0) * rate))` from the top two channels when `v1 > v0`, with `rate = (reaction / 100)^4` (`default reaction = 20`). Trace third-color quanta never stall two-color reaction, and slider values `10..40` sustain bright colored reactive zones while `100` annihilates overlap in one frame.

## source peak and total color limit (`field.js`, `source.js`, `tick.js`)

- Painting a source claims the cell exclusively for that player (`src[i] = 1 << p`); right-clicking clears any player's source on that cell (`src[i] = 0`).
- Sources hold peak color (`255`) unless constrained by per-cell cap (`capBase`, `capTerritory`) or player total-mass cap (`maxMass`, in `1000` display units, `0 = off`).
- When `maxMass > 0` and `depletion == 0`, injection halts at `budget[p] = maxMass * 1000 * FP_ONE` and gas equalizes across owned territory by Boyle's law (`pressure ≈ maxMass * 1000 / area`).

## panel controls (`controls.js`, `style.css`, `view.js`)

- `Allow mixing into other colors` checkbox sits directly above `Mix into other color` (`mixRate`) and `Mix reaction` (`reaction`), dimming both rows when unchecked. Dragging `Mix into other color` above `0` automatically enables `mixable` so moving the slider always takes immediate effect.
- `Total color limit` (`maxMass`) controls player color budget and displays `mass / limit` in `#stats`.

## validation

- `node experiments/checks.js` — 24 checks covering exact mass conservation, wall symmetry, pressure push, `mixRate` ordering, 3-species trace non-stall, visible reactive front (`mixed > 1500, bright > 10000`), `maxMass` equalization, and enemy-territory source purge.
- `node experiments/dom-smoke.js` — 17 checks covering boot, 11 sliders, grouped `#mixable` toggle, auto-enable on `mixRate` drag, paint/erase, tooltip, reset, and rAF loop.
- `node experiments/gas-bench.js` — `1.2–2.0 ms/frame` on `128×128`.

Manual pass: open `index.html`; observe unmixable R/G/B collision; check `Allow mixing into other colors` (or drag `Mix into other color`) and observe yellow/magenta/cyan overlap and steady reaction; sweep `Mix reaction` from `0%` to `80%`; set `Depletion` to `1.0/f` for radial gradients and back to `0.0/f` for equalization; set `Total color limit` to `400k` and watch mass cap and equalize.
