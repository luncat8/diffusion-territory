Implemented the capture-and-hold prototype and the objective GUI. The game is running as the **Game preview** (served on port 8000); `index.html` is also opened in the viewer.

## The objective
- Circular **center ring** (radius 24 cells, ~1 800 cells). Control = locally dominant pigment, reusing the existing 5% ownership rule, and you need **55% of the ring**.
- You play the **paint color** (R/G/B). Hold timer advances one frame per sim tick; losing control drains it at 2× so a brief flip doesn't instantly erase an attempt.
- Win state at the target time (10/20/30/60 s in the HUD, URL-persisted), best hold, captures, losses, lead changes, elapsed and in-control time are all tracked. Switching color or scene starts a new attempt.
- The ring itself is now a gauge: the arc fills in your color as you approach the target.

## Dynamic minimal GUI (new center column)
- **Hold bar + status** (CAPTURE / HOLDING / HELD), zone shares as numbers and a stacked R/G/B/open bar, margin to your strongest rival, trend in %/s, peak.
- **Attempt statistics**: best hold, captures, losses, lead flips, elapsed, in-control %, time the target was reached.
- **Contextual hint** derived from the same numbers: no pigment in the ring → paint it; below 55% → open mix/reaction; hold draining; holding with margin.
- **Focus block** surfaces only the controls that matter for this mode — production, pressure speed, viscosity, brush, mixing and reaction rate against each rival, reaction mode. They bind the same `Settings` as the full panel, relabel when you switch pigment, and the relevant ones get an amber accent for the current phase. Fold it with `–` to leave just the header/bar.

## Measurement (`npm run objective-bench`)
66 deterministic runs (6 existing scenes × 4 axes × 2 levels + 3 colors), each with the same opening claim:

| choice | mean control spread | mean peak-share spread |
|---|---|---|
| production 0 vs 16 | **1.2 s** | **7.6 pp** |
| pressure 20 vs 90 | 0.1 s | 0.0 pp |
| mixing closed vs open | 0.0 s | 0.7 pp |
| reaction off vs 20% | 0.5 s | 0.4 pp |
| your color | **2.8 s** | **31.4 pp** |

So currently production and color choice are meaningful; pressure, permeability and reaction are not yet levers on the ring within a 6 s attempt — and zero lead flips were recorded once the opening claim was in place. That is written up in `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`; the fix direction is scripted rival pressure plus scoring ring share continuously before retuning pressure/permeability.
