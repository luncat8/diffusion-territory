# 0.4.0 — capture-and-hold objective

The sandbox gains a goal: take the center ring with the selected pigment and keep at least 55 % of it for a set number of seconds. The ring is the only objective; painting, production, pressure, permeability and reaction remain the tools, so any rule change is judged by how it moves the ring contest.

## zone and control

- Ring at the map centre: `radius = 24` cells on 128², about 1 800 cells. The cell list is built once at boot.
- A cell belongs to a player when `Field.dom` names that player: local pigment dominance at or above the 5 % control threshold, ties and empty cells are open. The ring sample is one pass over the cached index list after `Field.analyze`.
- Control requires `need = ceil(area × 55 %)` cells of the ring. Shares are kept in percent.
- The ring carries the pigment mass of each player as a second, pressure-level readout. Mass answers "am I present" while share answers "do I own".

## attempt rules

- The active player is `Settings.paint`, the pigment the brush paints. Switching pigment starts a new attempt, so the HUD never compares a hold from one colour with a zone taken by another.
- The hold counter advances one frame per 60 Hz simulation tick while control is held. The target is `Settings.holdTarget` seconds (10, 20, 30 or 60 in the HUD, 5–120 in the URL), default 20.
- Losing control drains the hold at twice the fill rate. A drained counter returns to the capture state; the attempt keeps its best hold, captures, losses and lead changes.
- Reaching the target sets the won state and records the elapsed time of the attempt. A new attempt clears counters and history but leaves the map; reset reloads the scene and the rules.
- The target can change during an attempt: lowering it below the current hold wins immediately, raising it resumes the timer.
- Elapsed time counts simulation ticks, so pausing the simulation freezes the objective and its statistics.

## statistics

Attempt readouts, refreshed four times per second:

- hold progress, current hold seconds, target seconds, state (capture, holding, held);
- zone shares of all three pigments plus the open remainder, as numbers and as a stacked bar;
- the active player's share, the gap to the strongest rival, share trend in percent per second over the last half second, and the peak share of the attempt;
- best hold, captures (entries into control), losses (drops out of control), lead changes between controlling pigments;
- elapsed time, share of the attempt in control, time the target was reached;
- ring share change is sampled every 30 ticks against the previous sample, so the trend is a rate rather than a frame delta.

## HUD

- A compact column between the map and the settings panel, collapsible to its header and hold bar. It stays outside the canvas, so the ring is never covered.
- Header: objective name, three pigment buttons, state badge, collapse control.
- Body: hold bar in the active player's colour; hold line and zone line; stacked zone bar; detail lines for margin, trend and peak, then history and run time; one contextual hint line.
- Focus block: the controls that matter for the objective, each bound to the same setting as the full panel: cell production rate, pressure speed, viscosity, brush size, mixing and reaction rate against each rival, and the reaction mode. Rival rows follow the selected pigment, so both rows always describe the two opponents of the current colour.
- The block highlights the two or three controls that match the current state: brush and production when the ring holds no pigment, production, mixing and reaction while below the requirement, viscosity, reaction and mixing while holding.
- The hint is derived from the same numbers the bars show: no pigment in the ring, below the requirement, hold draining, holding with a margin, target held.
- Goal target and new attempt live in the HUD footer. A new attempt keeps the map; reset in the main panel reloads the scene.

## map feedback

The ring is stroked on the canvas above the pigment layer. Its arc fills clockwise from the top in the active player's colour as the hold approaches the target, so progress is readable without looking at the panel.

## measurement

`experiments/objective-bench.js` compares rule choices on the six existing scenes. Every variant gets the same opening move: clear a radius-8 disc at the centre and paint it with the active pigment. Scenes are re-seeded for each run; runs are deterministic.

Axes: pressure speed 20 vs 90, production 0 vs 16, mixing closed vs open at 60 %, reaction off vs 20 % with mixing open, plus the three pigment choices at scene defaults. Metrics per run: best hold seconds, total seconds in control, peak ring share, lead changes, captures. A six-second run with a three-second target keeps the hold from saturating on every scene.

Output is one fixed-key line per run and per-scene and per-axis spreads, for example:

	scene=2 axis=mixing level=open hold_s=3.0 control_s=4.1 share=71.2 flips=1 captures=2
	axis=reaction scenes=6 mean_hold_spread_s=1.4 mean_control_spread_s=1.7 mean_share_spread_pp=6.3 sensitive=1

An axis is marked sensitive when its mean control-time spread across scenes reaches one second or its mean peak-share spread reaches five points; control time is clamped at zero below the requirement, so the share spread keeps a losing scene from hiding a rule effect. Experiments are node-only and never loaded by the page.

## validation

- `node experiments/objective-checks.js` (part of `npm run checks`): zone geometry and need count, control detection, target win, decay on loss, zero-hold return, pigment switch reset, trend sign and magnitude, non-player control, target retuning.
- `node experiments/dom-smoke.js`: HUD present, zone initialised, focus controls bound to the main panel, pigment switch, target select and URL, hold status and bar after a painted ring, folding.
- Manual pass: boot the page, watch the ring and HUD while the scene runs; paint into the ring and watch share, trend and hint change; switch pigment and check that the attempt restarts and rival rows relabel; change the target; fold and unfold; new attempt keeps the map; reset reloads the scene; reload the URL and confirm target and pigment restore.
