	block ratio ~1.0 on a random smoothed blob (pyramid showed 5–50). wall leak R past a G column: 0 leaked cells in 200 frames.

decisions and why — the only part anyone rereads
	value restriction, not residual restriction: the A e = r coarse solve stalls (~0.93 contraction per cycle — the sz-weighted coarse operator is ~sz× stiffer per level on the homogeneous problem). The V-cycle flattens the coarse representation itself and prolongs the delta; same masks, edges, 2 sweeps per level.
	slider is a compute budget: pressure=100 buys 6 V-cycles per frame (MG_CYCLES_AT_100), weak settings accumulate fractional cycles, so a quiet field costs ~0.1–0.3 ms and 100% costs ~2 ms.
	mass is exact by construction: edge flows cancel pairwise at every level, restriction is a size-weighted average, and the per-frame rounding leftover is dumped to the richest cell of the channel; settle() cleans any deep debt. walls are exact by mask; coarsening splits 2×2 blocks along internal edges so no edge ever crosses a wall at any level.

pitfalls: per-slot visited reset, not per component, in the coarsen BFS (a per-component reset re-processes every cell four times and every aggregate lands in its own component).
next step suggestion: FMG warm start — skip re-solve cycles when the field changed by less than a threshold since the last pressure pass.
